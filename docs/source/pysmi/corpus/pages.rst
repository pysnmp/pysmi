The OID tree's page inventory
-----------------------------

Which arcs of the registration tree get a page of their own, and where the
rest resolve. A page per OID a corpus defines is 95,603 pages over the corpus
pysnmp/mibs publishes; the arcs above the modules are about 1,347 of them, and
that is the set worth rendering.
